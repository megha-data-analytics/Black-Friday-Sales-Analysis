# Black Friday Sales Analysis – End-to-End Data Analytics Capstone

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Black Friday retail transaction data to understand customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, and sales patterns.

The objective is to transform raw transaction data into meaningful business insights and recommendations that can help retailers improve marketing strategies, customer retention, and inventory planning.

## Dataset Information
- **Dataset:** Black Friday Sales Data
- **Records:** 550,068 transactions
- **Columns:** 12
- **Target Variable:** Purchase

### Key Features
- Customer demographics: Gender, Age, Occupation, Marital Status
- Geography: City Category, Stay in Current City Years
- Product information: Product ID, Product Categories
- Sales information: Purchase Amount

## Key Business KPIs
- Total Sales
- Average Purchase per Customer
- Repeat Purchase Rate
- Category Penetration
- City-wise Sales Contribution
- High-Value Customer Share

## Business Objectives
- Identify high-value customer segments.
- Understand purchasing behaviour across demographics.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Compare sales contributions across city categories.
- Discover cross-selling and upselling opportunities.
- Recommend customer retention and marketing strategies.
